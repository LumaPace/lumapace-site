Questions

Good to know

What is LumaPace?

LumaPace is a set of calm guided meditations spoken in clear, natural English. You relax, follow live captions phrase by phrase, and learn the key words from each session.

What level do I need?

If you can follow simple spoken English, you are ready. Live captions and word explanations help you along, and you can slow the audio down.

How long does it take each day?

Sessions are about 5 to 14 minutes long. You choose a daily listening goal that fits your day.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Two full sessions are free, with no account and no card. Premium unlocks every session.

How do I cancel?

Anytime. Web subscriptions can be cancelled from the subscription portal linked in your confirmation email. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, in your phone’s browser at lumapace.com. The Android app is coming soon to Google Play, and your progress syncs when you sign in.
