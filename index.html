Guided sessions

Relax while your ear learns.

Each episode is a calm, spoken meditation — a quiet garden, rain on a cabin roof. Follow the captions or just close your eyes and listen.

Words in context

Keep the words that matter to you.

Learn vocabulary the way you meet it in real life: inside a sentence, with meaning. Saved words come back for review at the right time.

Practice

Check what you understood.

Quick listening checks after each session, word reviews, and speaking practice that uses your device's speech recognition — your voice is never recorded or uploaded.

Your rhythm

Small daily goals, steady progress.

Set a daily listening goal, keep your streak and watch your level grow. Sign in once and your progress follows you between the app and the website.
