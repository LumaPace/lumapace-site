Your data

Delete your LumaPace account

This page explains how to delete your LumaPace (“English Through Meditation”) account and the data linked to it. It applies to the LumaPace Android app and website, developed by LumaPace.

Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to englishmeditation26@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my LumaPace account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to LumaPace, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Delete in the app

Open LumaPace → Profile → Delete account, then confirm. You need to be signed in. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — manage it in Google Play.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address, user ID and display name) in Firebase Authentication.
  • All synced learning progress: completed sessions, listening minutes, streak, saved words, review schedule, last listening position, listening-check results and app settings.

What may be kept

  • Subscription records. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Google keeps its own payment records under its policies, and RevenueCat may keep anonymized transaction records needed for accounting and fraud prevention.
  • Data on your device. Progress saved locally stays on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Questions? Email englishmeditation26@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
